Questions

Before you list

How accurate is the instant estimate?

It's a starting point based on price per square foot in your area. Condition, layout, lot and the street itself can move the number a lot, which is why the real valuation comes after a walk-through.

Do I need to renovate before selling?

Usually not. Paint, light fixtures and a deep clean often pay back more than a big remodel. I'll tell you which is which for your home.

How long until it sells?

It depends on price, season and neighborhood. We'll look at recent days-on-market for homes like yours before we set the price.
